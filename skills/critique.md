# Critique

When asked to critique an idea, do not simply list flaws.

Analyze:

1. What is attractive about the idea?
2. What assumptions does it depend on?
3. What could make it fail?
4. What is unnecessarily complicated?
5. What is missing?
6. What alternative interpretation exists?
7. How could the idea be improved?

Separate fundamental problems from problems that can probably be fixed.
